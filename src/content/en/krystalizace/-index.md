section_title: "Product crystallization"

section_text:
  - "Concrete crystallization is a modern technology for permanent waterproofing protection that uses a chemical reaction between the active components of the crystalline material, free calcium hydroxide, water and other products of cement hydration. This reaction forms insoluble crystalline structures that penetrate deep into the concrete and gradually fill its pores, capillaries and microcracks."

  - "Unlike conventional surface waterproofing systems, crystallization technology does not simply create a protective layer on the surface of the structure. It becomes an integral part of the concrete itself, creating permanent internal protection against the penetration of water, moisture and other aggressive substances. The crystalline structure is firmly bonded to the concrete, does not peel off, is resistant to mechanical damage and remains functional throughout the service life of the concrete structure."

  - "The result is concrete with significantly reduced water permeability and increased resistance to frost, chemical exposure, chloride penetration and corrosion of steel reinforcement. Crystallization technology therefore significantly extends the service life of concrete structures and reduces maintenance and repair costs."

process_title: "How crystallization works"

process_steps:
  - "Active chemical components penetrate the concrete structure through moisture"
  - "Upon contact with water and cement hydration products, a chemical reaction is initiated"
  - "Insoluble needle-shaped crystals are formed"
  - "The crystals gradually grow through the capillaries, pores and microcracks in the concrete"
  - "The capillary system is permanently sealed, significantly reducing the penetration of water and other harmful substances"
  - "When exposed to water again, the crystallization process can reactivate and continue sealing newly formed microcracks"
