**### Advanced protection for concrete structures**

Natrix is a modern crystalline waterproofing system designed for long-term protection of concrete and reinforced concrete structures against water, moisture and degradation. Its active components penetrate deep into the concrete structure, where they form insoluble crystals that fill pores and microcracks.

This technology reduces water permeability while increasing the structure's resistance to adverse environmental conditions. The system helps protect concrete against long-term deterioration and reinforcement corrosion while reducing the risk of further damage.

Natrix is suitable for both new construction and renovation projects and provides a reliable solution wherever long service life, high durability and effective waterproofing are essential.

**### Applications**

The product is ideal for foundations, underground structures, basements, retaining walls, tanks, tunnels and other concrete surfaces exposed to water or increased moisture.

Application is simple and efficient using either a roller or spray equipment. The active components react with moisture within the concrete and form a protective crystalline structure.

**### Key benefits**

Crystalline technology provides permanent protection without the need for additional waterproofing layers. The result is increased watertightness, improved protection of reinforcement against corrosion and an extended service life of the structure.

Natrix provides a reliable solution for professional concrete protection in modern construction.
