### Crystallization during concrete production

Prix is a crystalline admixture designed to be added directly to fresh concrete. During cement hydration, its active components form insoluble crystals that permanently seal pores and capillaries throughout the concrete mass.

The result is a watertight concrete structure with increased resistance to water penetration, other aggressive substances, and long-term exposure to moisture.

Prix is ideal for new concrete structures where long-term watertightness is required without the use of additional membranes.

### Applications

The product is used in the construction of foundation slabs, retaining walls, underground structures, water tanks, tunnels, elevator shafts, and other reinforced concrete structures exposed to water.

It is added directly to the fresh concrete mix during concrete production or mixing. The uniform distribution of active components creates protection throughout the entire volume of the structure.

### Key benefits

Crystalline technology creates permanent watertight protection without the need for additional waterproofing layers. The product extends the service life of concrete, protects reinforcement against corrosion, and helps reduce future repair and maintenance costs.
